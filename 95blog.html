the world feels lifeless. Except,
birds fly above me and settle on grey wires, fish probably
wait in a stream somewhere, there are of course
insects,
but to what I see, lately there are only the cold sides of buildings,
the emptiness of cities which don't end,
just lead to cold meadows, then trees, then again to the same.

it is as it is, my job is simply to continue in my way, the same as
everybody else.
my job is to walk down the street, to continue on to work, to not
stop and consider the birds on the wire. How it really
doesn't matter to them,
that I kept my head looking forward.

It is Sunday and no one is in the shop,
a steel-rimmed clock is looking down at me above
the door,
I'm sure I put it there but now I
hardly remember. It could have been someone else entirely
who stood on the rickety chair and
placed it there.
As it stands I can barely read the time,
it's too high up and the numbers blur together
so I must focus very hard to read it.

From the height of the sun it is
about mid-day and I go to sit
outside for a moment, watching
the hazy motion of passersby
drifting around like clouds
groups merging and then stringing out
across the sidewalk, no one comes nearer,
the velvet leaf
of a plant near the wall
catches my eye it's soft purple
reminding me of skin,
curling up from the dirt
and I
think of O'Keeffe suddenly
without knowing my hand already
to the soft fibers

looking around the shop

he is concealing something

he never looks at me
only down at the leaves
but in a far-off way I know
he won't buy anything
in a few moments he turns
back to the door quickly
the bell rings
once more

her soft skin completely forgotten
