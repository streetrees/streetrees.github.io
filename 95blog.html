He has looked at them many times and finds her interest in them pleasing. She rolls her suitcase slowly to the small window, and asks for a room in the common language of the city. He looks back at her a moment before going to the back wall where many keys hang in perfect rows on wooden bands. He finds the one he wants and returns to the small window. He thinks it is really like the hole in a stable where perhaps a horse would be resting. He wonders which is the animal, him or her? She has not moved from the small hole, her face illuminated in a sickly way by the far light.
